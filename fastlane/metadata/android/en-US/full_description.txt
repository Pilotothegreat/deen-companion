Deen Companion brings prayer times, the Quran, hadith, the Qibla and tasbih together in one calm, private app. No accounts, ads or trackers.

Prayer times and alerts
- Oman Ministry of Endowments timetable by default, plus Umm al-Qura, Muslim World League, ISNA, Egypt, Karachi, Ja'fari and Tehran
- Standard or Hanafi Asr
- Adhan and iqama notifications, with iqama as minutes after the adhan or a fixed time
- Next-prayer widget with a live countdown

Quran
- 604-page Madinah mushaf in the Uthmanic Hafs script with the Saheeh International translation
- Search that ignores diacritics, bookmarks and "continue reading"
- Ayah-by-ayah recitation with a sleep timer

Hadith
- Six major collections, with full collections downloaded only when you ask
- Favorites, grades and search

Qibla and tasbih
- Compass with calibration help and turn guidance
- Tasbih counter with the post-prayer 33/33/34 cycle and a home-screen widget

Material 3 Expressive design, dynamic color, light, dark and pure-black themes, in English and Arabic.
