Bilal is a calm, private companion for prayer, remembrance and the Quran, named after Bilal ibn Rabah, the first muezzin. There are no accounts, ads or trackers, and nearly everything works offline.

PRAYER TIMES AND ALERTS
• Accurate times with 14 calculation methods, including Oman's Ministry of Endowments, Umm al-Qura, Dubai, Kuwait, Qatar, Singapore, Turkey and the Moonsighting Committee
• The method follows your country automatically, or choose your own
• Standard or Hanafi Asr, per-prayer fine-tuning and high-latitude rules
• Adhan and iqama notifications, with iqama as minutes after the adhan or a fixed time
• The middle and last third of the night for qiyam

ATHKAR
• Morning and evening, after prayer, sleep and waking, and 31 everyday situations
• Each dhikr with its count, virtue and source
• A big tap counter, a daily streak and optional morning and evening reminders
• The right athkar suggested for the time of day

QURAN
• The 604-page Madinah mushaf in the Uthmanic Hafs script, with the Saheeh International translation
• Search that ignores diacritics, bookmarks and continue reading
• Ayah-by-ayah recitation by Mishary Alafasy, Mahmoud Khalil Al-Husary or Abdul Basit, with a sleep timer
• A verse of the day that opens in the mushaf

HADITH
• Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Jami' at-Tirmidhi, Sunan Abu Dawud, Sunan an-Nasa'i and Sunan Ibn Majah
• A sample of each is included; download full collections when you want them
• A daily hadith or prophetic dua

WIDGETS
• The next prayer with a live countdown
• Today's prayer times, the verse of the day, the daily hadith, athkar progress and a tasbih counter

AND MORE
• Qibla compass with calibration help and the distance to Makkah
• Offline search of about 11,600 cities in English and Arabic, with travel detection
• Material 3 Expressive design with dynamic colour and light, dark and pure-black themes
• English and Arabic, with a full right-to-left layout

Your location and settings stay on your device. Bilal is free and open source (GPL-3.0).
