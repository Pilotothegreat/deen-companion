🕌 Deen Companion

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 17, 2026
Short version: Deen Companion is designed to protect your privacy by default.
  • No personal data is ever collected
  • No accounts required
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking SDKs
  • Location is used only locally on your device
  • You are in full control

1. Who We Are

Deen Companion is developed and maintained by Piloto the Great (GitHub: Pilotothegreat). This is an open-source project. The source code is publicly available for inspection.

2. Data We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. The app runs entirely offline except for the optional features listed below.

Data Type Collected? Details
Name, email, account info Never No accounts exist
Location (GPS) Not stored Used locally on-device only to calculate prayer times and Qibla direction. Never transmitted.
Usage analytics Never No analytics SDK is included
Crash reports Never No crash reporting service
Advertising identifiers Never No ads in the app
Quran audio (streaming) Optional Streamed from public Quran audio CDN. No personal data sent.
IP-based location Optional Used as a fallback if GPS is unavailable and you enable this setting. Can be disabled.

3. Permissions Explained

Permission Why It's Needed
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION		 To calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla direction based on your current location. The location is processed on-device and never leaves your phone.
INTERNET For optional Quran audio streaming and optional IP-based location fallback. The app is fully functional offline without these.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS To deliver Adhan and Iqama reminders at the correct prayer times.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM
USE_EXACT_ALARM		 Required so prayer alarms fire at the exact scheduled second, not delayed by Android's default alarm batching.
REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS Prevents Android from killing scheduled prayer alarms during battery saving modes. Without this, alarms may be delayed or missed entirely.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Reschedules prayer alarms after the device is restarted so you don't miss them.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK		 Allows Quran audio to continue playing in the background with media controls in the notification shade.
VIBRATE For optional haptic feedback during Adhan/Iqama alerts.

4. Third-Party Services

Deen Companion does not include any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK, Crashlytics).

Optional network requests:

  • Quran audio: Streams from a public Islamic audio CDN (e.g., everyayah.com or similar). No personal data is included in requests.
  • IP geolocation: When GPS is unavailable and the user opts in, a public IP geolocation API may be queried. This sends your IP address to the service but no other personal information. This feature can be disabled in Settings.

5. Data Storage

All app data (prayer settings, Quran bookmarks, Hadith favorites, Tasbih counts, preferences) is stored locally on your device using Android's standard storage APIs (DataStore, Room database). This data is never uploaded to any server.

6. Children's Privacy

Deen Companion does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. No personal data is collected from any user regardless of age.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the new version will be published at this URL with a new "Last updated" date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on GitHub.

8. Open Source
