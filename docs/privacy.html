Deen Companion

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

In short: no accounts, ads, analytics or crash reporting. Your location, settings, bookmarks and favorites stay on your device. The app goes online only for the optional features listed below.

What stays on your device

  • Your location coordinates and city name, used for prayer times and the Qibla direction.
  • Your settings: calculation method, iqama times, notification choices, theme, language and Quran text size.
  • Quran bookmarks, your last-read page, hadith favorites and your tasbih count.

This data is stored locally with Android's DataStore and a Room database. It is never uploaded and is excluded from Android cloud backups.

Banking apps

The optional "Support development" sheet can open Omani banking apps if they are installed. The app only checks whether they are installed so it can show a button, and never sees any financial information.
